A 0-100 editorial score, not a Google metric: 30% commercial/lead intent, 25% local/service fit, 20% specificity and likely conversion, 15% observed SERP opening, 10% strength of the current destination page.

Local SEO action plan

Google states that local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence. The site can improve relevance and prominence, but it cannot optimize away distance. A complete, verified Google Business Profile is a core acquisition channel.

Week 1 - entity and profile

Confirm the primary brand/domain; verify GBP; use the correct service-area business setup; complete categories, phone, website, hours, services, description, and service areas; align name, phone, and site across citations.
