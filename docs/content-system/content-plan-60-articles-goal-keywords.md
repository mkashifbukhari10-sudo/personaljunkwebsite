# Goal-keyword plan — 10 targets, 48 areas, 60 supporting articles

**Written:** 2026-10-29. **This file does not publish anything** and it does not edit `keywords.md` or any other existing file.

**Supersedes the approach in** `docs/content-system/content-plan-60-articles-30-days.md` (pull request #5, not on `main` when this was written). That plan refused new URLs because the head terms were already owned. This plan keeps that rule for the 10 targets, then adds 60 supporting articles with **different** intents, plus an area-page strategy. Where the two files disagree, follow this one.

**Calendar:** two articles a day, Friday 2026-10-30 through Saturday 2026-11-28.

**Search volumes, difficulty and rank positions:** not measured. Observations below are pages that appeared in web-search results on 2026-10-29. This was not a Dubai-IP browser, so the local map pack was only partly visible.

---

## How ranking is split

Three kinds of URL, and they must not swap jobs.

| Intent | Who owns it | What must not happen |
|---|---|---|
| Generic target, no neighbourhood | The one page named in the map below | A second service page or a blog post aimed at the same phrase |
| Target plus a community | That community’s `/areas/{slug}` page | A new “junk removal in Marina” article, or the same paragraph pasted across 48 areas |
| A specific problem, item or deadline | One new or existing article | An article whose keyword is a target, or a word-order variant of a target |

Articles may link to an area page only when the sentence is about that community for a reason already in the area copy. No list of areas. No invented gate rules, lift sizes or “most residents” claims.

## Area ranking

**Rule:** `[target or its cluster] + [community]` belongs to `/areas/{slug}`. There are 48 area pages. Do not add 48 articles or 480 doorway URLs.

What each area page should own:

- **Cluster A in that place** (`junk removal`, `Dubai junk removal`, `junk removal service`, `junk collection` + area). One H1 already in the form “Junk removal in {area} …”. Do not stuff the other three into the H1.
- **Cluster B in that place** (`rubbish removal`, `trash pickup` + area). One sentence on the same area page, pointing at `/services/junk-removal`. Not a new URL.
- **Furniture removal + area.** Only if the page already talks about sofas, beds, villas or move-outs. One link to `/services/furniture-removal`. Marina, JLT, JBR, Downtown, JVC, Palm, Dubai Hills and Arabian Ranches already do this in substance. Do not paste a furniture block onto an area whose copy never mentions furniture.
- **Bulky waste collection + area.** One sentence: check the municipal guide for that address, then link `/blog/dubai-municipality-bulky-waste`. Do not write a new list of excluded communities. Palm and Discovery Gardens are already named in official and secondary bulky-waste descriptions; do not extend that list from memory.
- **Cardboard + area.** Only where the page already discusses moves, offices or warehouses. Safe existing hooks: Dubai Marina and JLT (furnished move-outs), JBR (short lets), Business Bay and DIFC (offices), Al Quoz and DIP (warehouses; DIP’s page already says cardboard from warehouses). Link `/blog/packing-material-disposal-dubai` and `/services/junk-removal`. No other area gets a cardboard paragraph.
- **Waste collection service + area.** Only where the page is already commercial or industrial: Al Quoz, DIP, Dubai South, DIFC. Link `/services/waste-removal`. Do not add contract-waste copy to villa communities (Arabian Ranches, Emirates Hills, The Springs, and the rest).

### Area pages that need on-page work first

These 18 pages in `content/areas-new.js` have an empty `seo.title`: DIFC, City Walk, Emirates Hills, Bluewaters, JVT, Arjan, Meydan, Dubai South, Jumeirah Park, Jumeirah Islands, The Lakes, DAMAC Hills 2, Mudon, Remraam, Al Qusais, Muhaisnah, Al Khawaneej, Dubai Festival City.

For each, set a unique title from the **existing** H1, 60 characters or fewer, no brand suffix, no pipe and no list of the 10 keywords. Do not invent a new local fact to fill the title. The body copy is already specific; the title is the gap.

The older area pages already have specific titles (Marina’s is about the service lift, Discovery Gardens about small loads, Al Quoz about warehouses). Leave those H1s. Add only the single missing sentence from the list above if it is true of that page’s current copy.

### What articles may do for areas

An article links to **one** area page only when the problem is the situation that page already describes. The calendar marks those links. Every other article stays city-wide and links only to the owner service or guide.

### What not to record as success

Impressions on an article for its own narrow keyword are not a win for the 10 targets. A win is the **owner URL** gaining impressions for the target, or the **area URL** gaining impressions for the target plus that area. Positions without those URLs do not count.
