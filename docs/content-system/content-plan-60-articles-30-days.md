## Methodology and limits

**Checked:** 2026-10-29.

**Sources**

- Every published article slug in `article-registry.md`, the service and area URLs above, `publishing-plan-2026-10-26.md`, `next-three-article-briefs.md`, and the six admission tests in `keywords.md`.
- Web search result sets. The page or snippet actually returned is named in the slot that uses it.

**Not available — say “not measured” rather than estimate**

- Search volume, difficulty, CPC, clicks and rank position. There is no Keyword Planner or Search Console export in the repository.
- A full Dubai local pack. This was not a Dubai-IP browser session. “Appeared in the result set” is not a rank.

**Competitor claims are not our facts**

Junkaway’s homepage says Dubai jobs confirmed before 2pm are same-day, and that 84 percent of what they collect is recycled, donated or resold. Amwaj’s homepage shows a 4.9 rating from 187 reviews, a 92 percent diversion claim, and a 2–4 hour response for calls before 2pm. JunkExperts shows mattress removal from AED 250 and sofa removal from AED 300. Quick Junk Remover shows paid removal from AED 99. None of those figures may be added to this site.

**The six tests**

1. Genuinely new — not an existing primary, secondary or wording variant.
2. Dubai and in scope — not buying, selling, scrap, moving-as-a-service, cleaning or another excluded intent.
3. The searcher wants something taken away or a clearance decided.
4. One destination.
5. No material fight with a service page, an area page or a published article.
6. A new URL only when the job is different enough to stand alone. A different wording is not enough.

`NO NEW URL` and `DO NOT WRITE` fail at least one test, or they are maintenance. `HOLD — DO NOT DRAFT` might pass later. Until the named fact arrives, the factual gate in `content-rules.md` stops the draft.

**Images.** A cleared article still has to reuse a key already in `content/file-posts.js`. There is no honest piano, pool-table, safe or hot-tub image in that library. Missing art is a second block after capability.
