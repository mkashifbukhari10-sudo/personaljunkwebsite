The foam left after an appliance delivery is its own pile. It is not the appliance, and it is not a stack of cartons. Bag the shapes, the loose beads and the film where they can be seen, and keep every part that still belongs to the machine out of those bags.

A booking that says “boxes from the delivery” will be read as cardboard. If half the volume is foam, the photo and the note have to say so before anyone prices the visit or plans the carry.

## Read what the retailer actually wrote

Open the delivery message and look for a line about packaging. Some teams take the carton and the inserts away. Some take the carton only. Some take nothing. Silence is not a promise that the foam will be collected on a later pass.

Copy the words they used. If the note says the packaging will be collected, write the day they named and leave that material intact until they have been. Do not flatten, cut or bag it into a junk pile while a take-back is still open. A second booking for the same foam is how the same pieces get described twice.

If the note says they do not take packaging, or it says nothing, the foam is yours to deal with. Add it to your own list. Do not wait for a van that was never going to return.

A verbal “they usually take it” is not the note. If you cannot find the sentence, write “not stated” and decide the foam is staying until you have an answer in writing. Asking takes one message. Guessing fills the room.

Keep the delivery note with the photos you will send. The person looking at a white shape on the floor cannot tell whether that shape is still promised to the retailer.

## Three groups, not one delivery

Stand everything that came with the appliance on a clear patch of floor. Sort it before you call any of it waste.

The first group is the foam: shaped inserts, corner blocks, a moulded tray, and any loose beads that have already escaped. This is the subject of this page.

The second group is the board: the carton, the inner sleeve, any cardboard tray. Flatten it only after it is empty. The way a move sorts cardboard, and the test for board that is wet or coated, already live on the [packing-material guide](/blog/packing-material-disposal-dubai). Use that page when the pile is a house move. Use it for the carton in this delivery only as the method for the board. Do not send the foam there to be described as boxes.

The third group is parts of the appliance you are keeping, or of an old appliance that is leaving. A shelf, a hose, a tray, a manual, a bag of bolts, a remote and a power lead are not foam because they were in the same carton. Put them with the machine they belong to. A shelf bagged as packaging is a shelf you will not have when you come to fit it.

Film, straps and tape are a fourth handful, not a reason to rebuild the groups. Roll the film into its own bag. A loose sheet on the floor will be walked through the beads.

If you cannot point at three separate groups, you have not looked yet. A closed carton with the foam still inside is one unknown object.

## Shapes, beads and film behave differently

A shaped insert has a count. Two side blocks, one top tray and one base are four pieces. Write the count. A sentence that says “some polystyrene” will be read as a small bag, including when the shapes fill a corner of the room.

Stack the shapes so the count can be seen in one photo. Do not nest them so tightly that the photo looks like one block. Do not crush them into a carton to hide the volume. Crushed foam springs back, and the volume you hid is the volume that has to be carried.

Loose beads are the part that escapes. If a moulded shape has cracked, the beads are already on the floor, in the carton flaps and in the hallway. Sweep them into a bag before you move the carton. A later sweep after the carton has gone still finds beads in the same places.

Put beads in a bag you can close, then put that bag inside a second bag if the first is thin. Do not sit on the bag to make it smaller. A split bag of beads in a stair or a lift is a longer job than the foam you started with. Do not tip beads into a sack of kitchen rubbish. They will be walked through the building, and the sack stops being a sack of ordinary waste.

Film and strapping cut the hands of the person who picks them up loose. Roll them. A strap with a metal buckle stays visible, not tucked under a shape where it will be grabbed blind.

A timber crate, a pallet or a nailed frame that arrived with a heavier appliance is not foam. Photograph the nails and the broken edges. Keep it beside the bags, named as a crate. Do not slide it under the carton and call the whole thing packaging.

## The appliance is a different list

The machine that just arrived is staying, unless you have decided otherwise. Do not add it to the foam booking because it is standing next to the foam. Name it only so the reader of the photo knows the white appliance in the background is not part of the load.

If an older machine is leaving as well, that machine has its own preparation. Several unlike appliances, and the order they leave in, are the [old-appliance register](/blog/old-appliance-removal-dubai). Stay on that page for disconnection, draining and the route of the units. Come back here for the foam those units arrived in. A fridge that still holds food is not made ready by bagging the inserts from the new one.

Do not cut a cable, pull a pipe or tip a machine so the foam is easier to reach. Move the bags out from around the appliance. If a shape is trapped behind a machine you are keeping, say so and leave the shape until the machine can be eased forward without forcing a connection. Write “one insert still behind the fridge” rather than pretending the count is complete.

Parts you are keeping stay with the machine after the foam has gone. Walk back once. A hose you meant to fit should not be in a bag that has left.

## A television’s foam is not the screen

A new television often arrives with more shaped foam than a small appliance, and the screen is a different object from those shapes. Where the set goes, whether it works, and how the glass is protected, is the [television guide](/blog/tv-disposal-dubai). This page does not move a screen and does not restate the electronics routes.

If the old set is leaving and the new one’s foam is staying, say both in the same message only so they are not mixed. The foam booking is the bags. The screen booking is the set. A carton that still has the television in it is not empty packaging.

The inserts that were keeping a screen safe can be bagged once the set is out and standing where you want it. Do not bag the screen to make the foam pile look like one item. Do not tape foam to the glass and call that protection you invented on the day. If the original carton is the protection the set still needs, leave that carton with the set and say it is not part of the foam load.

Brackets, a stand and a bag of screws from the television carton belong with the set, same as a shelf belongs with a fridge. Check the flaps before you flatten.

## Not a move, and not a stack of stock cartons

This pile exists because one appliance, or a short run of appliances, was delivered to a home. It is not the cardboard left at the end of a house move. If you are unpacking a whole move, stay on the packing guide and treat this foam as one line inside that larger sort. Do not open a second booking that describes the same bags.

If the cartons held goods you sell, and the foam is an extra line beside those cartons, the subject is the [product-box note](/blog/product-boxes-not-moving-boxes-dubai). That page keeps the goods out of the pile. This page starts when the foam from an appliance in a home is the thing you need taken.

If the flat is filling with successive delivery cartons and you still live there, the volume of board is the [shipping-carton plan](/blog/shipping-cartons-filling-a-flat-dubai). Foam in that flat is still a separate line. When the line has become the whole problem, because an appliance’s inserts are what will not fit anywhere, you are on this page. Do not rename it “boxes” to make it sound smaller.

The bins guide already places packing foam with the materials it keeps out of a recyclables container. This page does not add a bin rule and does not promise a container that will take the bags. Follow the bins guide for the container. Use this page to get the foam into bags that match the photos.

## Bag it where it cannot spill

Use bags you can lift. A shape that is larger than a bag can be stacked and taped into a bundle you can point at, with a written count on the top piece. Do not break shapes into beads so they fit a sack. Broken shapes become the loose fill you were trying to avoid.

Label the bags in pen: “foam shapes”, “beads”, “film”. A bag with no label will be opened on the day by someone who has to ask what it is.

Keep the bags indoors, in the room or the hall you control. A corridor, a lift lobby or the mouth of a chute is not a waiting place unless the building has told you that place may be used. If it has not, the bags stay inside. A neighbour’s complaint is not a collection.

Do not post a bag of foam into a chute to see if it will go. If it jams, you have a blockage and you still have the rest of the pile. If it drops, you have not made a plan for the shapes that would not fit. The chute is not a measurement of what a collection will take.

Keep beads away from a balcony drain and an air-conditioning drip. Wet foam sticks to the bag and to the floor, and the weight changes. If a bag is already wet or dirty, say so. Do not hide it under dry shapes.

If a child or a pet is in the home, the beads are on the floor until they are in the closed bag. Close the bag before you leave the room to take the photo from the doorway. An open bag in the photo is an open bag in the route.

## Photograph the foam, not a closed carton

Send three photos at minimum.

The first is the shapes, spread or stacked so the count can be checked against your note. Include a doorway or a skirting in the frame so the size is obvious. A close-up of one corner block hides the pile.

The second is the bead bag and the film bag, closed and labelled, with any crate beside them. If there is no crate, do not invent one in the note.

The third is the carton, empty and flattened, or still assembled if you have not emptied it yet. If you have not emptied it, you are early. Empty it before you ask for a time.

Add the appliance in the background only if you label it: “fridge staying” or “old washer also leaving, separate list”. A wide photo of a kitchen with a new fridge and a heap of foam will be read as both unless you say which is which.

Show the door the bags will leave by. If the building has already told you which lift or which stair, copy that instruction. If it has not, write “no lift instruction yet”. Do not invent a booking. One building’s rule is not the next building’s rule.

Take the photos in room light. White foam against a bright window becomes a blank. Step so the edges of the shapes are visible.

## Book the foam as its own line

[Take the foam with the other junk](/services/junk-removal) once the bags are real and the appliance is either staying or listed on its own. This is the packaging. It is not a collection of the machine, and it is not a cardboard route.

In the message, say:

- that the load is foam from an appliance delivery, and the count of shapes
- whether there is a bag of beads, a bag of film, or both
- what you did with the carton
- whether the retailer is still due to take any of it, in their words, or “not stated”
- the appliance that arrived, and that it is staying or that it is a separate list
- any old machine that is also leaving, by name, or that there is none
- anything still trapped behind a machine
- the floor and the door, and any lift note you already have
- that the bags are inside the home

Do not write “packaging” as the only line. Name the foam. A photo of a closed carton does not show the beads.

If the carton is the larger pile and the foam is one small bag, say that too. The small bag still needs its own words, or it will be assumed not to exist. The packing guide remains the place for a move’s board. This note remains the foam.

If you have not checked the flaps for a shelf, a hose or a bag of fixings, you are early. Those parts are not waste until you know the machine does not need them.

The old-appliance page remains the machines. The television page remains the screen. The packing guide remains the move and the board.

## On the morning it leaves

Put the labelled bags where the photos showed them. Do not push them into a cupboard after the photos were taken. Point at the appliance that is staying so it is not lifted with the shapes. Point at any part you kept, so a shelf is not picked up as foam.

Walk once from the bags to the door. A flattened carton you left in the turn is part of the route. Move it onto the pile or out of the way before anyone lifts.

If the retailer arrives after all and takes the packaging, tell the collection before it is on the way. A visit booked for bags that have already gone is the overlap you can still cancel. If they take the carton and leave the beads, the beads are still this job. Say what is left, with a new photo if the pile has changed.

Do not carry the bags down to a lobby to save time inside the flat unless the building has said that lobby may hold them. If it has not, they wait in the room.

After the bags have gone, look behind the appliance and along the skirting for beads you missed. A second handful is a new note if someone has to return for it. It is not part of the visit that already left. Sweep it up before you call the room finished.

The useful record is the count you wrote at the start: how many shapes, whether the beads were bagged, and that the machine was never inside the bag.
