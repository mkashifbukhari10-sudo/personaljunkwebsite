A leak has soaked the cardboard. Keep that board off the dry stacks you still hope a recycling route will take, name the water, and book the soaked pile as junk once you know it is waste. A route that asks for dry board can refuse a wet bundle. Drying it on the balcony does not turn it back into the description you would have sent yesterday.

A photo of one stained corner does not show how much of the stack collapsed, or what else the water reached.

## Split wet board from dry board

Start with the pile the water actually touched. Pull every carton that is still dry, still firm, and still yours to recycle away from the soaked sheets. Do not flatten a wet carton onto a dry bundle to make one tidy photo. The dry sheets stay a recycling question. The wet sheets are a different line on the same note.

Work from the bottom. Water sits in the lowest layer, in a carton that was against an outside wall, and in the sheet that was left under an air-conditioning pipe. A carton that looks dry on the logo side can be pulp underneath. Lift the corner. If it bends and stays bent, or if it leaves a wet mark on the floor, it belongs with the wet line.

Do not tape a wet carton shut to hide the stain. A closed box that smells, drips, or falls apart in a corridor is harder to carry than an open wet sheet you have already set aside. If the carton is empty, open it fully, let it drain where the water will not run into a neighbour’s store, and stand it where it cannot soak the dry pile. If it still holds something, deal with the contents before you call the carton waste. A warranty, a cable, or the item itself is not packaging.

Leave mover-owned cartons, crates and blankets out of both piles until you have read the agreement. A wet carton that the mover expects back is their problem to hear about, not a sheet you cut up. The packing guide says the same for a dry move. It applies here because water does not transfer ownership.

If only the bottom of a tall stack is wet, do not write “all the boxes are wet”. Count the wet ones you can see and say the rest of the stack was lifted off them. A quote built on a whole-room description will not match one spoiled layer plus a dry stack.

## Say where the water came from

Write one line for the source, as far as you can see it. An air-conditioning drip, rain on an open balcony, a pipe under a sink, a spilled drink, or water coming from the flat above are different notes. You do not have to diagnose the building. You do have to stop adding dry board to the wet floor, and you do have to say whether the water is still arriving.

If the drip is still running, do not build the outgoing pile under it. Move the wet board to a dry part of the same room, or to a room the water has not reached, and say the leak is active. A collection cannot be the repair. Ask the building or the person responsible for the pipe to deal with the source. Photograph the drip and the wet floor so the note shows why the board is no longer a neat stack.

If the water came from a drain, a toilet, or a container of paint, solvent or pesticide, do not bag that board as ordinary cardboard. Photograph the source and the label. The [acceptance guide](/blog/what-we-take-dubai) is where sewage-soiled material, liquid chemicals and gas cylinders are separated from household junk. Ask about those materials before they go into a taped carton. This page does not add a new list.

A balcony carton that caught rain is still this page if the board is soaked. One damp sheet among a flat full of dry delivery cartons is a line on the [shipping-carton plan](/blog/shipping-cartons-filling-a-flat-dubai), which already tells you to keep that sheet off the dry stack. Stay on this page when the wet board is the problem you are trying to clear, not a footnote on a room of dry deliveries.

Do not stand wet sheets where wind can lift them off a rail. Wet board is heavier than dry board and it tears. A sheet that leaves your balcony is not a recycling success. Bring it inside or weigh it down in a place you control, away from the dry pile.

## Leave the recycling test on the packing guide

The [packing-material guide](/blog/packing-material-disposal-dubai) owns the sort after a move: what to flatten, what a building container can take, and the Municipality’s test for board that is food-soiled or coated with plastic. Read it if the cartons are the remains of a move and the only issue is volume. Come back here when a leak is why the board cannot sit with the dry sheets.

Dubai Municipality’s [Waste Segregation Guide](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/12/Waste-Segregation-Guide.pdf) describes a green container for recyclable dry mixed materials, asks that cardboard be flattened, asks that food contamination be removed from boxes, and excludes cardboard that is contaminated with food or coated with plastic. Where a building uses bags, the guide calls for clear bags so the contents can be seen. Cardboard that is wet from a leak, and is not food-soiled or plastic-coated, is not named in that exclusion. Ask the building what its route will accept. Do not place the soaked sheets in the dry container while you wait.

A recycling pickup that has asked you for dry, flattened board can refuse the wet bundle. That refusal is a reason to keep the piles apart. It is not a rule you copy onto every building in the city. Get the answer for this address. If the answer is yes, follow the conditions they give you, including any limit on how much they will take. If the answer is no, or if they do not answer before the board starts to break down, the wet line is a junk load.

Do not invent a second bin rule on this page. Do not rinse a food-stained pizza box and call the leak solved. Food-soiled board stays out of the recyclables stream under the guide already cited. A leak that only wet clean cartons is a different fact. Say which one you have. A stack can contain both: clean cartons the drip soaked, and a food-stained box that was already unfit for the container. Split those two as well.

Drying the sheets in the sun does not rewrite the note by itself. If you spread them out, say they were soaked, say whether they are dry now, and say whether they tore or grew mould while they sat. A brittle sheet that was pulp yesterday is not the same object as a carton you flattened on move-in day. Ask the route again if you want that sheet in the recycling container. Do not mix it back into the dry bundle in silence.

## A lived-in delivery pile is not this page

Successive dry cartons from sofas, appliances and online orders, in a flat you still live in, belong on the shipping-carton plan. That page is the volume, the keep-versus-discard decision, and the path through rooms you still use. Use it when the board is dry. Use this page when water has changed the board.

If both are true — a room of dry delivery cartons, and a soaked layer from a drip — send two descriptions. The dry room follows the shipping plan. The wet layer follows this one. Do not average them into “boxes”.

Office cardboard, shop packaging and cartons mixed with tiles or gypsum are other jobs. The [office-waste guide](/blog/office-waste-collection-dubai), the [shop-clearance guide](/blog/shop-clearance-dubai) and the [renovation-waste guide](/blog/renovation-waste-removal-dubai) own those premises. A leak in a flat does not become a shop clear-out because the carton once held a product. A leak on a fit-out, where the soaked board is mixed with offcuts, is the renovation case below, not a household stack with a new name.

Foam corners, plastic film and a timber crate that got wet do not become cardboard. Pull them out and give them a line. A crate with nails stays a crate. The packing guide already treats those materials as their own description. Follow that. Water does not merge them.

## Book the wet pile as junk

Once the soaked board is empty and yours to discard, and a recycling route has refused it or cannot take it before it falls apart, it can travel with the other junk. The junk-removal page says flattened cardboard can ride in a junk load, and that the page is not a cardboard recycling route. Wet board is the same booking type with a different description. It is not a free recycling contract, and it is not a reason to hide the sheet under a dry bundle.

[Ask for a junk load if the cardboard is wet](/services/junk-removal). In the same message, list anything else that is leaving: the dry board you also cannot store, the broken chair, the rug. Say the board is wet. Say whether it is still in carton shape or already in torn sheets. Intact wet cartons hold water and air. Torn sheets take less space and still need a bag or a wrap so they do not shed pulp in the stair.

A soaked sofa, mattress or appliance is not this page. Do not assume it travels on the same terms as the board. Photograph it on its own line and ask before you add it. The acceptance guide lists ordinary household items and ordinary cardboard. It does not, in that list, settle a saturated upholstered piece. This article will not invent that answer. If the only thing that got wet is the packaging around a dry appliance, say so. The appliance then follows its own guide, and the wet carton stays here.

Keep the wet pile off the route you still walk. Wet board on a landing drips onto the step and onto a neighbour’s door. Leave it inside until the crew is there, unless building management has told you to stage it in a named place. The [pre-arrival guide](/blog/before-the-crew-arrives-dubai) is the general check for a path and for what stays. Add the wet-or-dry line from this page, because a path photo that hides a dripping stack will not match the load.

If a retailer was still due to collect the carton, tell them it got wet before you cut it up. A returns carton is not junk while the return is open. Finish the return, or decide it is not happening, and then describe what is left. A crew cannot judge a returns label through a soaked flap.

Do not promise the building that the bin room will be clear of wet board tonight. Ask management before you put a dripping stack in a shared room. A few dry sheets may belong in their container. A wet pile that has collapsed is a collection, not a favour you do the chute.

## Soaked packaging on a renovation

Packaging that a leak soaked on a fit-out, mixed with offcuts, broken tiles or old fittings, is a project pile. It is not a household carton you flatten for a green container. The [renovation-waste guide](/blog/renovation-waste-removal-dubai) is the refit. For the booking itself, use a [project waste load](/services/waste-removal) when the pile is the site, not one room of delivery cartons in a flat you live in. That page is paid project and site waste. It is not a municipal bin contract.

Say what the water touched. A soaked carton beside dry offcuts can still be separated. A carton that has become one mass with wet plaster is a different object. Photograph the mass. Do not call it cardboard so that it sounds lighter than it is.

Household junk and a site pile can share a day only if you show both and the scope is confirmed as one visit or as two. Do not describe a skip’s worth of wet gypsum as “boxes from a leak”. The renovation guide keeps the site rules. This page keeps the soaked board.

## Photos that show the water

Send a wide photo of the wet pile from the doorway, then a closer photo of the drip, the stain, or the layer that tore. Include the dry stack in a separate frame so the two are not one lump. If the wet board is on a balcony, show the rail and the floor so the volume is visible without you leaning out.

Write these lines with the photos:

- Which room, and whether the leak is still active.
- How many cartons or sheets are wet, as far as you can see without climbing the stack.
- Whether they are empty.
- Food-stained, plastic-coated, or clean board that only got wet.
- Foam, film and timber listed apart from the board.
- Any sofa, mattress or appliance that is also wet, on its own line, not assumed.
- Paint, solvent, pesticide or drain water, if that is the source, kept out of the pile until you have asked.
- The floor, and whether the way out is a service lift, a passenger lift or stairs.

A logo close-up does not show weight. A dark patch does not show whether the sheet is still in one piece. If you cannot see the back of the pile, say the photo is the front only.

If there is no service lift, say so, and use the [walk-up stairs guide](/blog/walk-up-stairs-no-lift-dubai) for the flights. Wet sheets shed pulp on a turn. Do not stage them on the landing to save a trip. The stair guide says the load stays inside until the carry starts.

On a high floor, name the floor. Wet board is an awkward carry because it bends and drips, not because it is a wardrobe. The person quoting still needs the floor and the lift answer. “Apartment” is not a route.

## On the day the crew comes

Walk the dry pile and the wet pile before anything is lifted. Point at the wet line. If more water arrived after you sent the photos, say so at the start and send a new wide shot if the visit has not begun. A quote for one layer is the wrong plan for a stack that collapsed overnight.

Check the keep side. A carton you meant to save can sit against the wet sheets and look like waste. Move it, or mark it in the room, before the load starts. Open one outgoing carton if you are no longer sure it was empty. Papers, a passport copy and a remote are still easy to miss inside a flap that stuck shut as it dried.

After the wet board has gone, look at the floor where it stood. A ring of pulp is not a second load unless you have agreed to include cleaning, and this booking is not a cleaning contract. Wipe what you can if you want the floor clear, or leave the mark and do not describe the job as finished decoration. The crew’s scope is the material you listed.

If the building later accepts the dry sheets in its container, take them there under the conditions you were given. If it does not, they stay a second line on the junk request, described as dry. Do not add them to the wet bag on the way out so the photo from yesterday becomes false.

The last useful check is the source of the water. If the drip is still active, the next carton you stand there will join this pile. The collection removes the board you have already spoiled. It does not close the pipe.

## Questions

### Is wet cardboard the same job as packing material after a move?

No. The packing guide is the move: flattening, mover-owned cartons, and the Municipality test for food-soiled or plastic-coated board. This page starts when a leak has soaked the cardboard and it must be kept off the dry stack.

### Can I put the wet sheets in the building’s recycling container?

Ask the building. The Municipality guide cited above excludes food-soiled and plastic-coated cardboard, and it describes dry mixed recyclables. It does not name leak-soaked board as its own class. Use the answer you are given for this address. Do not add the wet sheets to a dry bundle while you wait.

### Will a junk booking take wet cardboard with other items?

Ask, and show the wet pile separately from the dry board and from any furniture. The junk-removal page allows flattened cardboard in a junk load. It is not a recycling contract. The request should say the board is wet.

### What if a sofa or mattress got wet as well?

Photograph it on its own and ask before you add it. This page is the cardboard. It does not decide a saturated sofa, mattress or appliance.

### What if the leak is still dripping?

Move the board out from under the drip, say the leak is active, and ask the building or the person responsible for the pipe to deal with the source. Book the board that is already waste. Do not treat the collection as the repair.

### Where do shop, office or fit-out cartons belong?

Not in a household note. Use the office, shop or renovation guide that matches the premises. Soaked packaging mixed with site offcuts is a project pile, booked as project waste, not as a flat’s delivery cartons.
